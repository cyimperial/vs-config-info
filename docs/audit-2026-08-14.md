### What each category measures

1. **Frontmatter & discoverability** — does `name` / `description` cause the skill to actually
   trigger? Third-person phrasing, concrete trigger phrases, under the 1024-char budget.
2. **Structure / progressive disclosure** — is `SKILL.md` a lean router, with detail in
   bundled files, or a monolith the model must read in full every time?
3. **Embedded code correctness** — does shipped code parse and run?
4. **Portability** — does it work for someone other than the author, on a different machine?
5. **Safety & side effects** — is destructive or expensive work opt-in and contained?
6. **Internal consistency** — do the instructions contradict each other or their own examples?
7. **Tool-API accuracy** — do referenced tool parameters actually exist?
8. **Repo hygiene** — licence, layout, docs.
